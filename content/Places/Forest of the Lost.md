The forest of the lost is a location in Faerûn that is shared with many villages and settlements that share stories of a forest that you should not venture to, normally accompanied by a tale or warning

# Current Sightings

As mentioned, the Forest of the Lost is a dynamic, shared location; there is no single "Forest," but a shared name and story. Thus, the party has found themselves in this forest on the following occasions:

## Gravebrook

The party discovered the forest of the lost in Gravebrook after speaking with Elda Harrow, the village Chief. He told them never to venture into the forest alone, as its proximity to the Fields of the Dead has led many adventurers astray, possibly because of the grave battles and foul arcane events that occurred here. He told them that if they wanted to navigate the Forest, they would need guidance from a magical item. This item was the token of seeking, which the party found on Finn, who had received it from a man named Nicholas, who said he had dropped it. This token took the party to the cave that ultimately led to a ritual site

### Area

The Gravebrook forest was noted for its silence and eerie atmosphere; it felt as though it was suffocating anyone who traveled through it. The party found the area withered and almost faded in color. This area seemed corrupted, with purple crystals as they ventured in. Inside the forest, they found corrupted goblins who had been enslaved by who they believed to be Nicholas, and also housed a cave in which they found the following creatures: Spiders, Shadows, Chupacabras and the Neo Chupacabra.

## Woodchuck Hamlet

At Woodchuck Hamlet, the party discovered the second instance of the Forest of the lost, still top be ventured by them, they were told by Wendy (the village healer) that they were always told to only take what you need from the forest, and not venture far as you would get lost. The party found a similar token, though this one having the image of a mask and flames
