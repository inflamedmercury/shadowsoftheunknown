### Oracle
- A unique entity, they are a Hellebor, seemingly capable of seeing the future, though they see all possibilities at the same time, currently being watched over by the Ravens and in their protection. Their powers although powerful, are difficult to control or use, they are constantly either confused or in pain if they concentrate too hard, thus they use dampening bracelets to suppress their powers. 
	- When having a conversation with someone, they might answer with something different, this is a result of them looking into a different timeline in which they were asked something else
	- Explained that what they see are the different "Strands" of the [[Weave]] that they see at the same time, they do not know which strand will ultimately be incorporated into the Weave, thus although seeing more than a divinator could, they are uncertain of what will happen
