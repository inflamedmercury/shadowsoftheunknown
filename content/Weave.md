The Weave is the unseen fabric of reality, a shimmering network of magical energy that permeates the world and connects all things. More than just the source of magic, it is tied to grounding reality in Faerûn, when tampered with anomalies occur. ## Mystra 

The Weave is intrinsically linked to Mystra, the goddess of magic. She maintains and guides its flow, ensuring the delicate balance of arcane power. Aiding in its balance are her followers, Clerics, Mages, and any individual who venerates her, the Weave is balance and order and thus it should not be messed with

## The Loom of Fate

The strands of the Weave aren’t static. They constantly shift and change, representing the endless possibilities of what _could_ be. Events are woven into the Weave only after they occur, solidifying their place in reality. The future isn’t fixed, but a tapestry of potential, waiting to be shaped.

## Self-Correction:

The Weave fiercely resists being torn or manipulated in unnatural ways. When someone attempts to damage or unravel it, the Weave instinctively attempts to repair itself. However, these disruptions sometimes create anomalies that are known as [[Unknowns, Hellebors or Anaithnids]]

### What affects the Weave

As time goes by, natural fractionations of magical energy can create rips in the weave, but these normally turn into wild-magic zones, in which spell casting is possible, but comes with a higher chance of an undesirable effect occuring. When occurring in a larger context, these cause the Hellebors to spawn, anomalies that do not follow the same rules of the material plane. These can manifest in different ways, resembling what it might have been but warped in nature

Another important factor is that of emotion and a creatures intent. In the same way the gods become more powerful the more creatures venerate them. When human emotions and desires are intense, these in combination with magic can create anomalies, some of these more stable than others, in best case scenarios these feelings turn into Anima, or vital energy which can empower, this is how [[Objects of Power]] are born. But in other occasions these can bring forth Hellebors, that are twisted from those emotions

Finally, purposeful tampering can occur, from what our party has learned, there are many ways to disrupt the weave with the required components. From their travels in Gravebrook, they found mana infused crystals that when used in rituals can forcefully create Hellebors. There seems to be organizations that create these anomalies on purpose.
