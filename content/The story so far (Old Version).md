
The story so far: important NPCs and events to remember

  

The party visited a village known as Gravebrook, a recent settlement found in the fields of the dead, an area known for the many conflicts that occurred in the past, leading to rumors of ghosts, ghouls, and monsters. The village had posted a help-wanted poster as their sheep had been curiously slaughtered in the night. The party arrives and investigates, meeting the village head elder, Elda Harrow, who pointed them to a recent carcass. Of note, he had spoken of how Wilhelm’s wife, Solana, had visited them at some point and helped them cure the ailing and use her magic to fix things around town. The party had found a sheep that had been killed and drained of all of its blood. Brawyn, using his knowledge, had noted that it had no signs of a violent scuffle, but had an X-shaped incision, indicative of an animal that sucked blood, but he had never seen such a big wound of this type. The party investigated the town, finding the stagehand, a dimwit by all measures, Finn, who stated that another villager, Stefan, was in charge of looking for the sheep that night. The party found Stefan, and ultimately Ka-Veh shat his pants using prestidigitation, which persuaded Stefan to tell the truth: that he had actually unloaded his duties to Finn so that he could meet with his lover, the Elder's daughter.

The party goes to Finn’s house, where although he admits to being there at that night, reveals that he had not been present, as he was doing “something”, this something was a ritual, which was done using a sheep as sacrifice, Wilhelm disturbed the circle via the pouring of some water, which ultimately sucked up the party into a zone, filled with shadowy figures who tried to kill the heroes, they fought them off finding strange purple crystals in the ritual sit. They then questioned Finn, who admitted that he was promised great powers to fulfill his adventuring dreams by a man named Nicholas. The party continued to investigate and rested when suddenly one of the sheep was kidnapped, and Stefan chased after it. The party followed behind him, and when they found themselves lost in the woods, suddenly feeling a pulse from a charm that the Elder had given them, they found their way into a twisted version of a forest.

In said forest, the party found themselves attacked by a party of goblins, who looked possessed and in pain. In the middle of combat, Willhelm and Ka-Veh realized that a corrupting force was controlling them. The goblins, thankful, told them that a man who was wearing a dark robe had done something to them. The goblin party escorted the players to a cave, where they saw many crystals similar to the ones in the basement but of a natural variety. They fought a group of corrupted spiders and started to investigate, finding runes and magical residue among the caverns. As they passed a row of crystals, each member saw a frightening sight, with Ka-Veh and Magnus receiving the brunt of psychological damage, fearing their lack of freedom and inadequacies, respectively.  They recovered when suddenly Brawyn's journal began glowing, showing entries related to the creatures they had confronted and some Unknown creatures. They heard Stefan's voice when they were confronted by the chupacabra and a humanoid chupacabra who had Stefan's voice, with a pale and knocked-out Stefan on the floor. They fought and beat these, with the Alpha Chupacabra starting to gain speech and understanding before ultimately being defeated. Of note, a perfectly black lotus was found by Ka-Veh, whose patron Ea-Nasir asked for, crafting and giving Ka-Veh a deck of cards whose faces changed every time drawn.

Wilhelm found a disturbing journal that had cryptic messages by Nicholas; inside, he finds scribbles of a madman, eventually mentioning Solana and a person or entity called “The Unweaver.” Wilhelm uses an enigmatic spell that had appeared in his spellbook, Whispers of the Unknown, where he peers into events that took place in the caverns; he sees Nicholas working through his experiments and a sight of his wife. This caused an emotional outburst that seemed to activate the crystal ritual site once again, which in turn caused a cave-in. The party narrowly escapes as they find the raven guard at the mouth of the cave, and at its helm, Magnus's father, Sir Chord. who, although stern in his memory, embraces the adventurers, but worries that his escape meant his true calling had been discovered.

As the party goes back to town, they find that Gravebrook is nowhere to be found, instead replaced by a lone farm housing an older gentleman who said that his son Finn had disappeared years ago. Confused by this, the party was convinced by Sir Chord to return with him, although a messenger raven arrived, requiring his presence. Although he volunteered to leave part of his men as escorts, the party refused and traveled alone. The party encountered Ea-Nasir, who revealed that he had some knowledge of Nicholas. They entered his vessel, where the party played games to entertain the Djinn, ultimately besting his games. The party gained some items, and Wilhelm saw a vision of his wife wearing a white cloak bearing the symbol of Mystra, which brought him relief. Ea-Nasir admitted to knowing of Nicholas, who was going to enter into a pact with him, but ultimately did not, finding an alternative to gain his desires. He tells the party that, that’s what they could get from him without entering a pact, and reminding Ka Veh that if he asks for anything, an exchange must occur, though tells him that he is much more important than he realizes, stating “ Our meeting was not purely luck, perhaps you are much more stronger than you thought, but leave the scheming to me”. The party then went on to travel to Baldur’s Gate.

On the way, the party camped, and during his watch, Brawyn found tracks that led him to a crystal-infused owlbear. He followed it closely until the owlbear left, letting its presence be known, leaving behind a crystallized feather, which Brawyn took. Upon arriving at Balders Gate, they find the city much more populated than before. As they make their way to where Sir Chord told them to be found, they find themselves in an illusion, being pushed around by the crowd until they reach an alleyway. In this alleyway, a monster attacks the party, specifically Brawyn, seemingly targeting his journal, which spews its contents; the monster is accompanied by corrupted Blink dogs, a fey creature thought to be extinct. Using illusory magic, the monster kept trying to take Brawyn's tome but eventually was felled. Before the people are aware of what is happening, a robbed figure arrives with a distinctive lapel pin bearing a netherise symbol and a circle on the inside. They talk to the party, presenting themselves as Lucille, a red-haired half-elf, a member of the Society of the Unknown, who intended to swipe Brawyn's Journal. Before she can do so, Sir Chord arrives, grabbing her hand and stating that “Artifacts are not to be stolen, even in these circumstances.”  She smiles and retreats

Sir Chord apologizes for what has happened and takes the party to the headquarters of the [[Society of the Unknown]] ; the teleportation makes Magnus and Brawyn Puke. In the headquarters, Sir Chord explains that the Raven Guard knight ship is a front for the Raven faction of the Society of the Unknown, paladins of Mystra tasked with maintaining the Weave. Explaining that the Unknowns, or creatures that were found, were strange entities born from conflicts in the weave, “spat” out as the weave tried to correct them, and laced with a primal and mostly unknown force that is arcane, yet little is understood. The society is in charge of making sure these do not harm anyone, but they are separated into different factions.

The Ravens – Duty-bound paladins and knights of Mystra, who protect the weave by bringing order when Unknowns are present. They do so through different means, viewing unknowns as a product of the Weave; they take caution and look at each one case by case

The Copper Circle – Cunning members who leverage information to gain an upper hand; they run logistics and are in charge of the manufacturing of possible Unknown repellent and experimental gear. They view Unknowns as an opportunity to either gain a profit or gain information.

The Eyes – Antiquarians, Lorekeepers and Historians; they are in charge of documenting and studying the Unknowns. They view them purely through an analytical lens, making sure to gain as much information as possible for them

The Swords – Monster Hunters and warriors tasked with the capture and/or execution of  Unknowns deemed dangerous to the populace; they only think of Unknowns as entities they must put under control one way or the other.

The Shadowguard – A secretive faction, in charge of internal affairs, making sure that no one faction gains more power than the other; these are tasked with making sure that balance is maintained, and hunt for traitors. Normally not present at the headquarters, or so it is told.

He introduces them to what had needed his attention: an unknown named “Oracle” (retconned from Many Eyes), who was a talkative and intelligent creature of the Unknown, able to see the feature via the exploration of the different “Strands” that make up the Weave. They also meet Elara, a Raven, who is the researcher. Using her glasses artifact, she can tell that the Unknown has touched the party in different ways. She is hesitant to unshackle Oracle, but the Unknown eventually relents, seeing visions of what had happened at Gravebrook and tales of Nicholas; he does this before collapsing. The party is invited to join the Society of the Unknown as members, with the choice of joining any of the factions at any point if they wished so; their first mission being investigating the disappearance of a Copper shipment, copper being the metal that, when attuned and manufactured in a certain way, serves as a general weakness to the Unknowns. Meanwhile, Elara was given the Nicholas Journal to be investigated.

The party is tipped to an area that has had “cult activities” that was more than likely where the copper was stolen. As they investigate, the party finds a woman who was working as a lookout but eventually confesses to being threatened by some robbed figures to help with a heist, offering payment for her help and secrecy. Wilhelm assures her that they will protect her and save her from whoever it is. Brawyn becomes drunk and starts to dance. The woman points them to a specific place before Wilhelm tells her to go to a militia barracks, where she will be protected. They find an alleyway where Magnus and Wilhelm find traces of boxes being moved; meanwhile, Ea Nasir, for some reason, asks Ka-Veh to trade one of his coppers with a copper that a kid nearby is playing with. Discovering that this copper piece was somehow “wrong,” they find that the robbers paid the kid off not to say anything; ultimately, the kid is entranced with Ea Nasir, and his fate is Unknown.

Following these traces, the party finds an alleyway where they find the residue of magical graffiti showing a symbol of a broken eye, pointing towards an abandoned bathhouse. As they investigate the bathhouse, they find that this is merely an illusion, seeing in its stead a recently built fort. Inside the fort, Brawyn discovers a trap door, which he manages to open, but using too much force, ends up falling into a hole, where they find themselves face to face with a member of the cult performing a ritual. The party gets into combat when the members protect their leader, who is close to a crystal very similar to those in Gravebrook, surrounded by copper. The ritual culminates with an Unknown monster appearing, covered in eyes and detesting being seen. The party eventually defeats it and makes the cultist leader surrender. They interrogate the leader, finding out that the ritual was done as a way to stabilize the summoning ritual they had found at Gravebrook, at a much smaller scale, with the copper being a stabilizing agent. The leader cannot speak of his master, as his tongue is shown to have runes that prohibit him from talking. A maddened  Wilhelm frightens the man until eventually he bites his own tongue; a spell is cast upon him that prevents his spirit from leaving for 10 days. 

In the cave, they find a pixie called Peridot Bluevale who had been gagged by the members, but does not remember her current predicament, and decides to follow the party for now. During this time, the party discovers a scroll that mentions that all results should be sent to Warehouse 7. A fact that eludes the party: Ka-Veh, because he only knows of 6 houses for ports; Magnus and Wilhelm, because they know that years ago this warehouse was burned down. Magnus remembers a foggy memory of his father whispering about this incident, stating that he was wary of what he was told, and that the clerics would not let him pass. Wilhelm remembers that night, with a great fog, only remembering that he was on evacuation duty and that his wife, Solana, was there. Wilhelm decides to use his Whispers of the Unknown, which fill his mind with visions of the fires, the burning, the fact that his wife was not there the whole night, the disappearance of everything, and feeling himself being overtaken by some force. Ka-Veh, trying to stop Wilhelm, accidentally peers into his mind, protected from the damage, but hears whispers of what had happened.

Peridot, scared, informs Wilhelm that the spell he had just used was primeval in nature and not attuned to the Weave; Brawyn could taste the spell. The party returns to the Headquarters, where Sir Chords is initially confused about the Warehouse 7 incident, recounting his experience but baffled that he cannot remember. The scrolls stated that the results should be sent to the Warehouse at Full moon, which was ten days away. After some persuasion, the party can gain access to the reports. They go to sleep, and this is the story so far
