# Full Session Recap

The party started, walking through the town of Baldur's Gate, as they discussed with Mr. Wood, what they would do, taking on the 5000 Gold Bounty of eradicating the anomaly that was occurring at the Woodchuck Hamlet

## At [[The City of Baldur's Gate]]

The party was about to depart when [Lucille](Lucille) showed up and shared a dossier with them that included all the information on the Swords from the [Society of the Unknown](Society%20of%20the%20Unknown). It contained a detailed account of the Hamlet, noting that the owner (at least at the time) was Charles Wood ([Chuck Wood](Chuck%20Wood.md)'s father). It was bordering on becoming a town, with an estimated population of about 150–200 people. The Hamlet dedicates itself to wood chopping. The Swords indicate that scouting units have detected fluctuations in the area, with energy signatures being consistent with the plane of fire. The Swords recommend taking action and taking anti-flame measures before heading there. An addendum was added at the bottom stating that all resources had been rerouted due to the Gravebrook incident and were thus put on hold.

Lucille says she decided to find this information as a favor to the party and as an apology for her first encounter with them (trying to steal Brawyn's book). Willhelm takes the scroll, and they continue on their way. At the gates, [Chuck Wood](Chuck%20Wood.md) introduces the party to his Stagecoach, Saver. A well-built dwarf, red-headed with a beautiful beard. He instructs the Stagecoach to take the party to the Hamlet, with Saver saying, "Welp, 4th time's the charm, eh?" [Chuck](Chuck%20Wood.md) tells the party he will be in Baldur's Gate for a bit to get some paperwork done to secure his new seat at the [Parliament of Peers](Parliament%20of%20Peers.md), which he inherited after his father passed away. He asked the party if there was anything they wanted him to investigate at the Parliament; the party decided to talk about it after they finished investigating. As the party entered, Saver squinted at Ka-Veh as he got on, noticing his copper skin.

## On the Road Again

On the way to the Hamlet, little happened on the trail, except the party seemed to spot a creature they swore they had seen before at Baldur's Gate; since it seemed inconsequential, they paid it no mind. As they kept going along the road, Saver started small talk with the party, beginning with Wilhelm and learning a bit about his background. Intrigued that he was from Amn, he asked if he had ventured south before, and Wilhelm said he had not. He moved on to Magnus, who stated that he was from [The City of Baldur's Gate](The%20City%20of%20Baldur's%20Gate.md), born and raised. He then quickly moved on to Ka-Veh, making a point to talk to him. After learning he was from the Calimsham Empire, he began questioning him about his past, and Ka-Veh deceived the dwarf with a made-up backstory. Eventually things turned tense, as he began talking of the recent slave-traders that had been talked about in the Baldurian Area, asking if he was interested in any of the supposed bounties on the escaped slaves. Ka-Veh responded, "Yes, I will make sure to report any of those to make a quick buck." Upon saying this, the Cart stopped, and Saver changed expressions, looking directly at Ka-Veh.

He firmly told him that if this was true, he could not, in good faith, take him to the Hamlet, a haven for outcasts, runaways, and those seeking a new beginning. Understanding this was a misunderstanding, Ka-Veh assured Saver that he was not looking for slaves and would keep everything professional. Being convinced, Saver apologized for his behavior, citing that Charles Wood had made the Hamlet a safe place for those who needed a new home, a place for new beginnings. He lamented his passing and noted that [Chuck](Chuck%20Wood.md) had to take the post during a turbulent time. With this resolved, the party continued to the Hamlet
## Arrival in the Hamlet

After a few hours of travel through the forest, they finally emerged into a clearing and saw Woodchuck Hamlet, a sprawling settlement with people of many races: Half-Elf, Human, Halfling, Elf, Half-Orc, among others. The area was next to a lake and a field that was the logging site, currently abandoned due to current events. One notable feature was the common rest area in the middle, which featured a curious-looking pyre. On the pyre, they could see soot and effigies. They noticed a little girl making an effigy doll and dressing it. Saver noted that this was part of their tradition, a ritual of sorts. The Effigy represented a person they loved and cared about but had lost; once finished, they would throw the effigies into the Pyre to symbolize letting go.

Saver introduced the party to the tavern keeper, where they could rest for the night. Before going to sleep, they decided to get some information. The tavern keeper talked about the events that had happened and how most of the adventurers sent had not come back, with only piles of soot and ash found, leading them to believe they had been incinerated. The tavern keeper tells the party that one survivor saw the creature, but he is currently being tended to and may be asleep; his name is Cinder. He also pointed to a strange traveler who was currently in the rest area.
## Masked Woman and a Fiery Welcome

At the rest area, the party finds a [mysterious figure](Masked%20Woman.md), slender and clad in a mix of dark leather and robes, wearing a purple-hewn mask, staring at the Pyre. From what the party gathered, she was admiring the Pyre, stating how beautiful it was, the actual fire and what it symbolizes. While she kept her gaze on the Pyre, only lifting her mask to drink some ale, Wilhelm began a conversation with her. The masked woman began to speak of how she found interest in this ritual, and how symbolically it seemed to mirror what was occurring in the forest: those who went in being taken by the flames, nothing left, being let go. The party tried to learn from the woman, only to learn she was a Cleric and didn't seem tied to any light- or justice-related pantheon. At this point, Brawyn decided to dance with the locals. As the conversation went on, the woman asked Wilhelm if something was ailing him; she seemed to intuit that he was burdened by something great, stating that she was just a nobody who was broken and could recognize someone else who was broken as well. This made the party uncomfortable. She stated that if he felt himself too taken aback by whatever ailed him, she could help him. Magnus questioned her intentions: what would she gain from this? She replied, "Is helping in itself not a reward?"

Meanwhile, Ka-Veh decided to investigate the nearby forest, using his Familiar, to which he was attuned in sight, sense, and feel, and sent it there. After some distance, through his familiar, Ka-Veh saw scorch marks, ash, and burning in the surrounding area; eventually, he was overwhelmed with feelings of loss, sadness, grief, and longing. Suddenly, something in the forest lashed at the Familiar, striking it with flames. Ka-Veh immediately told the party what he saw, felt, and what the attack was. With this, the party decided to retreat for the night, Ka-Veh recommending they get fire protection in the morning; Brawyn said he could slash the flames with his axe. Before leaving, the masked woman told the party, "If you ever feel too much pain to bear, the Night Mistress will be there for you." Magnus told her that Wilhelm was taken; he had a wife. Finally realizing that this was a Cleric of Shar The Party went to sleep.

## Morning and Cinder

After the fiery attack, the party went to the stalls and found a potion salesman, buying fire-resistance potions. In the morning, they went to Cinder's cabin and noted that he was a Flame Genasi; Ka-Veh felt bad about his jokes about Ifrits attacking the village. In the Cabin was the Healer Wendy, who had taken care of Cinder but noted that he was not physically ill, but mentally scarred. Wilhelm tried to determine whether this was magical in nature, but found no magical traces in Cinder's mind. He quickly noticed Cinder staring at the Forest from the cabin window. He and Brawyn moved his bed, noting that he kept his stare fixed at the Forest. Eventually, after being hindered from the sight of the Forest as Wilhelm closed the window, Cinder finally reacted, saying, "Irene! Don't go." This made Wendy begin to sob. She told the party that Irene was Cinder's wife, one of the sweetest Elves she had ever met. Both Cinder and Irene were escaped slaves from Calimsham, but unfortunately Irene had already died; thus, it was only fair for him to say this.

Outside the cabin, Magnus was investigating the forest, looking for anything that could give him a clue. However, after initial failure, with a little help from Peridot and her Fey magic, Magnus noticed something important. This forest had the same layout and Aura as the [Forest of the Lost](Forest%20of%20the%20Lost.md), the forest that housed the cave at Gravebrook. As the realization hit him, he felt something odd on his head; his whole scalp was now ablaze, his locks now strands of fire. This was an effect of the fey magic that he leaned on to intuit his latest finding. He conveyed this to the party, who then realized that Cinder had a closed fist. Wilhelm opened it with no effort, as the man had been enfeebled from lack of eating. In his hand was a token, similar to the one at Gravebrook, but with a theatre mask and a flame on it. Cinder feebly asked Wilhelm, "Please.... you must bring her back.... You must help her." Wilhelm solemnly told him that he would try

And thus the session ended.
