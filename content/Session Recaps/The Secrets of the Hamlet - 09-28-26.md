# Full session recap

## Old man Tom the Tavern and more Bullshit

After receiving the token from Cinder and learning that the Forest surrounding the village is similar to the Forest of the Lost in Gravebrook, the party decided to talk to the oldest villager. A man named Tom was found near the lake and told the party he had seen the tavern keeper (Brandem), Saver, and Charles go into the forest the Night of the first incident. The party flung the man into the river (sorry), and he disappeared.

After this, the party confronted Brandem, who told them that Calimshar slave traders had gone into the village and chased Irene into the Forest; that's when he, Saver, and Charles followed them. Unfortunately for them, they were too late, and the slavers had accidentally killed Irene. They tried to fend them off, but got overwhelmed. One of the slavers shot a bolt at Cinder, who was saved by Charles but was shot by a bolt. This caused Cinder to have an emotional outburst, ending with him summoning terrible-looking black flames that consumed not only the slavers but also Charles wood. They decided to take Cinder back to the Hamlet.

Upon returning to Hamlet, knowing Chuck could blame Cinder for his father's death, they decided to lie about the chain of events and tell the Village that a mysterious creature killed Charles. This turned out to be a terrible mistake, as the attacks continued to this day. He commented on the fact that Cinder was in the village the whole time, thus not being able to have committed these attacks

## Bad dreams and The Pyre <sup>tm</sup>

The party decided to visit Cinder again to figure things out. Once there, he was sleeping. Wilhelm woke him; Cinder had just had a nightmare. Wendy, in the room, heard about the nightmare and said she'd had the same dream. The party realized the village houses had dream-catchers. Wendy told the party that ever since Irene's death, they had all been having terrible dreams, and added that the pyre during Irene's ritual had glowed with an intense, ghastly fire. Wilhelm used detect magic and found remnants of [Hellebor](Unknowns,%20Hellebors%20or%20Anaithnids) magic.

The party went to the pyre, where they found an effigy that was buried in the ashes, an effigy of Irene that seemed to be intact. After studying it, Wilhelm realized it was covered in Unknown magic and tethered to the Forest. Knowing how rituals and worship work, the party surmised that whatever possessed this effigy did not want the ritual completed; it did not want the effigy burned; it did not want the village to let go. They went to the forest, where they found a mad Saver who had encountered the Shar worshipper. After finding her at the hinges of the Forest, she spoke of how the Forest was odd, and showed the party a feather, a strange mineral-covered feather, similar to the one that Brawyn had in his inventory from an encounter in the past. She left, saying her mistress wouldn't want her to go in there haphazardly, and warned the party that if they walked in, they might walk into certain death. Wilhelm said that if they saw each other again, he would like to talk, and he asked for her name; she smirked and said she was a nobody, but most called her "Poppy".

## Combat in the Forest and many Exploding Bones

Upon entering the Forest, Peridot (who was always there) commented that she felt as though they had entered the Feywild, or at the very least something similar. The party noticed that the Forest, though similar to the Forest of the Lost from Gravebrook, was not quiet; instead, it was much livelier and more colorful. They noticed charred bones on the ground; this was the location that Ka-Veh's Familiar had been to and had been attacked. They commented that it was much closer than expected, until they noticed trees surrounded them. Suddenly, the bones on the ground began to rattle and rearm as a spark of fire surrounded them. The party was attacked by fire bolts, and combat began.

Seeing the reconstructed skeletons and the fire that enveloped them, they spotted 4 adventurers they intuited were part of the parties that had gone missing. During the battle, as they attacked the creatures, they noticed their flesh was made of fire, and their attacks exposed the bones beneath. Once defeated, the fires returned to the core of the skeletons and blew up; this spelled disaster for Magnus, who took the brunt of the attacks but survived. After defeating these creatures, the party noticed a figure in the distance walking away; each member saw something different. To Wilhelm, he saw Solana, his wife, his everything; she was finally here. To Brawyn, he saw a creature that he had thought was lost, an Almiraj whom he nursed back to health but who had gone missing: Pokey. To Ka-Veh, he saw a woman whose name he did not know, a fellow slave who cared for him almost as a son. To Magnus, he saw... someone he missed dearly

And thus the session ended.
