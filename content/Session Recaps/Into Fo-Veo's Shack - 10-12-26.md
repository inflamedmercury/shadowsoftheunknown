# Into the shack we go

As the party sees a figure that resembles the person they miss and love most, they enter a small shack and see someone who, to them, looks like someone they love. For Wilhelm, this is his Missing wife, Solana; for Ka-Veh, he sees a slave from the past that served as a maternal Figure for all other slaves; and Magnus, who was in a delirious state, had seen an old Crush of his, a love that never was. The figure was in their shack, seemingly washing clothes at the lake. Wilhelm immediately went to them, thinking that this was his wife, to which the figure said that they were not; they knew what it seemed, but they were not Solana. Wilhelm, disheartened, grabbed the figure by the arm as they walked toward the other party members; there was no resistance, only sadness and empathy. The figure told him that they would explain everything but that they should attend to Magnus's wounds, as he had almost fallen into combat. Defeated, Wilhelm let go as the figure transformed into the visage of the woman Magnus had thought about earlier. He took their hand and walked into the shack. As they walked, the figure kept changing into the corresponding person's most cherished and missed figure, going from Solana to Ka-Veh's mother figure, to Pookey for Brawyn, and a red-haired figure in a green dress for Peridot. As they entered the shack, the figure sat Magnus in a bed and started to prepare a medicinal brew for them.

# We ate soup

Ka-Veh stayed at the door frame, suspicious of the Figure. The figure offered the party a medicinal stew that filled them with warmth and cured their wounds, feeling completely rested. The figure introduced themselves as Fa-Veo, an entity from the Spirit Realm who, for some reason, had been bound to the Material Realm. Explaining that they had the ability, or curse, to take the shape of the person whom they cherished the most, they normally represented what the remembrance of those who had been lost was. Originally a denizen of the spiritual realm, they had been born from the ritual that the village had commenced, but some magical shifting’s (possibly Unknown or Hellebor energy) had caused them to be brought into the Material Realm, the current shack being a counterpart to their original dwellings in the spirit realm, explaining that things in the spiritual realm mirrored those that were in the material world but in a much grander and more beautiful scale. They then stated that they had a counterpart, a partner to them, called Sal-Vo; they took on the form of fire, representing letting go, this being analogous to the Pyre portion of the ritual. They stated that Sal-Vo had been corrupted and now ran rampant in the woods of Woodchuck Hamlet, and that this was the source of the attacks.

# The Chapel and sad vibes, my dude 

Fa-Veo then |ook the party to a chapel that was well taken care of but small in scale, once again stating that this was the material-plane equivalent of their dwellings in the spiritual plane. They showed them what was normally the cycle of normal worship for them, showing two tomes, one that was blank, and another one that was ablaze. When Wilhelm asked someone to step forward, he saw the blank tome fill with his most cherished memories of Solana, which made him sad. Then Fa-Veo explained that this would then transfer to the fire tome, symbolizing moving on. The Tome then took on a black, fiery aspect; Fa-Veo explained that something had become corrupted in recent times, and that the party had what they needed to release part of the curse: the Effigy that represented Irene. They started to see memories of Irene, as she was a Calimshar slave, how she survived those times, and had a Glimpse of something interesting: the woman that had raised Ka-Veh, and to Ka-Veh he was glimpses of himself before becoming a Genasi. They finally burned the effigy, releasing a burst of energy that made Fa-Veo fall. They then asked the party to release their partner from this torture; if they could find a way to uncorrupt them, then do so, but if not, release them from their curse. Fa-Veo finally gave them a tip that might be useful for some or worrying to others (I'm talking about Ka-Veh here): none of the persons (or creatures) she had taken form of had perished; the flames of their life still burned. This brought immense relief to Wilhelm.

# Off we go to the forest

Finally knowing what they might be facing, the party returned to their earlier battle site, where they could now investigate without being attacked by stray fire or enemies. They found various piles of ash, soot, and charred bones. Upon investigating, they found footsteps that Brawyn identified as a bugbear; they followed the trail, which led them to a cave. At the cave opening, the party noticed an off scent and identified it as sulfurous. It seemed to linger in the air, coming from the cave. The party then prepared themselves to enter the cave and face whatever waited for them...
